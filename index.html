About Me

✦

Well I told myself if 1 day I lost my memory, this will be the place I return and try to regain all my thoughts and memories from it.

My work is in low emission fuel, with a focus on producing biofuels for the transport sectors. What keeps me interested isn't just the product itself, but everything behind it: how a barrel of fuel is produced in refineries, traded across oceans, priced by markets, and burned to move a plane, a ship, or a truck. It is a commodity that can moves across the world to power a nation, and ultimately, to keep human civilization advancing.

I play badminton, gym, hike and cycle to keep myself sane, and play Dota2 from mental deterioration. And when the opportunity comes up, I perform with an orchestra as a flutist. I'm also an Formula 1 fan.
